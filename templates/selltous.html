  • ✦

    Pristine & Verified Authentic

    Items must be 100% genuine with proper documentation, original packaging, or structural brand tags attached.

  • ✦

    Premium Categories Only

    We actively accept high-end consumer electronics, designer apparel, luxury timepieces, and limited-edition lifestyle items.

    Scalable Partnerships

    While we welcome single high-value luxury assets, we prioritize vendors capable of consistent batch or overstock supply.
